What is a Warrant Canary?

A warrant canary is a method by which a service provider can indirectly inform users about government requests that they may be legally prohibited from disclosing directly. The concept is simple: as long as this page states we have NOT received certain types of requests, you can trust that we haven't. If this page is removed, not updated, or the statements change, you should assume the worst.

Why This Matters

Some government orders (like National Security Letters in the US) come with gag orders that prohibit companies from even acknowledging they received a request. While we cannot say "we received a request," we CAN choose to stop saying "we have NOT received a request." The absence of this canary is the signal.

Our Commitment

This canary will be updated monthly. If more than 60 days pass without an update, or if any of the statements above are removed or modified, you should consider the canary dead and act accordingly. Our architecture minimizes data collection, but transparency about legal pressure is equally important.
